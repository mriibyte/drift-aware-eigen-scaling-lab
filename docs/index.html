What is being tested?

ft(x) = ½ (x − xt*)TAt(x − xt*)
xt+1 = xt − ηt ∇ft(xt)
ηt = γ / ((1 + safety) λ̂max,t)
  • Normal GD keeps η fixed, so it cannot react when the high-curvature eigenvalue grows.
  • Frozen scaling uses spectral information once at the start, but becomes stale under drift.
  • Drift-aware scaling estimates λmax with power iteration, filters noise, and reacts faster to upward curvature changes.
  • Move the curvature drift slider high to make stale step sizes visibly unsafe.
